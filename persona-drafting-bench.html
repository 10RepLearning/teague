Persona-Based-Prompting Drafting Bench

Build a Persona-Based-Prompting (PBP) activity one station at a time. This interactive Drafting Bench will not write your persona or your prompt for you — it will read what you enter and will provide feedback on what it notices and why.

Station 1 — Instructional Goal

Name the concrete instructional objective and learner population this PBP activity needs to serve. Broad, unmeasurable goals make every later station harder to check.

Station 2 — Persona

Draft the persona itself: who is speaking, in what voice, with what expertise or constraints. A persona that could be swapped for any other without changing the activity is decoration, not instruction.

—
Fidelity signal: a heuristic read on specificity, not a grade. Revise and re-check as many times as you like.

Station 3 — Prompt Assembly

Combine the goal and persona into the actual prompt you would hand to an AI system — not a description of what it should do, but the working text itself.

Station 4 — Peer Fidelity Review

A peer runs your exported prompt against an AI and works through this checklist. This edition of the checklist is static for the beta trial — a future session will invite this cohort to add and refine items, becoming its founding editors.

No.Check
1Did the persona hold up under a follow-up question, or did it slip into generic AI voice partway through?
2Could this same output have come from a plain scenario-based (SBL) prompt with no persona at all? If so, what would need to change to make the persona necessary?
3Did the persona serve the author's stated goal, or was it decorative — present in tone but not shaping the substance of the response?
